Once a thriving trade hub built on a narrow, spindly peak whose upper reaches held great fire-beacon lighthouses to guide gliders and cable cars. A quake toppled one of the towers, severing vital links. The Roiling Fog rushed up the weakened rock, and now the peak’s upper halls are ghost-haunted and charred, their beacons long extinguished.

This should serve as a semi-mega dungeon - one large enough to return to a few times at least, but not intended for a full campaign to take place in.

I'm thinking 3 large towers with some smaller structures at their bases.  One of the towers is the one that collapsed, but the other two are still quite tall, albeit maybe missing some chunks.  The towers are connected at various points by bridges, zip-lines, and maybe even a small cable car or something.

I want to Xander the hell out of this dungeon - there should be several potential entry points (maybe some that don't open up till later), and numerous ways between the floors besides just taking the stairs.  There is probably a main staircase in each that was damaged/blocked at some points to prevent constant ascension.
