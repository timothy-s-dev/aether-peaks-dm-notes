Where the arcane and industrial spaces were found. This tower has almost entirely collapsed.  The ground level is covered in rubble, and the second and third are just small sections of floor, though one has an in-tact zip-line leaving it for the Tower of Cinders.

# Inhabitants
The Tower of Smoke has been long abandoned, the ghosts mostly gone, and too little shelter provided by the rubble for large animal life. The basement under the tower, however, is a different story.

The spirits here are panicked still, likely initially fleeing the party until/unless trapped, at which point they will fight like cornered animals. This fear and aggression has kept that section of the underground largely abandoned by living things, though there are signs that something was unleashed here that must have since escaped.
