Once a thriving trade hub built on a narrow, spindly peak whose upper reaches held great fire-beacon lighthouses to guide gliders and cable cars. A magical accident collapsed one of the towers, and resulted in the deaths of most of the inhabitants. Those who survived struggled to provide a single cohesive explanation, and the ruins are generally avoided by travelers, as the upper halls remain ghost-haunted and charred.

# The Towers
There were once three towers in Ashenspire, built vertically to make the most of the incredibly limited horizontal space:
* **The Tower of Ash**, where most of the administrative and civic spaces were found.
* **The Tower of Cinders**, where most of the residential and communcal spaces were found.
* **The Tower of Smoke**, where the arcane and industrial spaces were found.

The accident appears to have originated in the **Tower of Smoke**, as that's the tower that collapsed, but the exact nature of that accident has been lost to time.

The outsides of the towers were once covered in the arcane-hydroponic farms that fed the inhabitants, but all that has since rotted away, leaving only the original stone.  

# Inhabitants
Ashenspire has long been abandoned by the survivors of the accident, but many of the less fortunate remain. Whatever happened appears to have trapped the spirits of many or all of those who died here, and their ghosts remain, haunting the ruins. Some are aggressive, attacking "outsiders" on sight, while others merely repeat their daily tasks or final moments endlessly. Though even the latter group are still dangerous, as they generally won't bother going around people, and having one pass through you is an exceptionally painful experience.

But the ghosts don't quite have the tower to themselves. A flock of [[Fog Skimmers]] have roosted on the top of the **Tower of Cinders**, and hunt on the nearby mountain-sides. Additionally, where water has leaked in and caused rot in the areas impacted by the magical accident, [[Myconids]] have arisen and begun to make themselves comfortable.
# Navigation
Finding your way around the ruins is difficult - some passage ways have collapsed, other walls have crumbled making new entry ways. Additionally, a magical lock-down was triggered at some point during the emergency, and the various stair-wells were each blocked with a magical barrier, preventing easy traversal up and down the towers.

Some alternative routes remain - there are a few in-tact zip-lines between the buildings, a few vertical shafts that remain navigable, and collapsed walls have opened new pathways through the ruins.
