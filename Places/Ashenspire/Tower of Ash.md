Where most of the administrative and civic spaces were found. This space remains in surprisingly good repair, and wasn't wrecked as badly as the [[Tower of Cinders]] when the [[Tower of Smoke]] collapsed.

# Inhabitants
The spirits here are orderly, mostly stepping through their daily tasks as if nothing had changed. Most will ignore the party unless they're "in the way", or somewhere they "aren't supposed to be".  And if the party can intuit what the ghosts want (move, go back to public spaces, etc) they'll generally leave them alone.

There are exceptions - the spirits in the hidden Claw section of the tower are unlikely to be comfortable leaving survivors who are aware of their hideout, and will pursue the party relentlessly.

## The Bound Magistrate
Another exception is the Bound Magistrate - anyone who reaches him is, by necessity, trespassing, and needs to be punished to the fullest extent of the law.
