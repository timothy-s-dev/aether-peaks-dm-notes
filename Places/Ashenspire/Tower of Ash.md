Where most of the administrative and civic spaces were found. This space remains in surprisingly good repair, and wasn't wrecked as badly as the [[Tower of Cinders]] when the [[Tower of Smoke]] collapsed.

# Inhabitants
The spirits here are orderly, mostly stepping through their daily tasks as if nothing had changed. Most will ignore the party unless they're "in the way", or somewhere they "aren't supposed to be".  And if the party can intuit what the ghosts want (move, go back to public spaces, etc) they'll generally leave them alone.

There are exceptions - the spirits in the hidden Claw section of the tower are unlikely to be comfortable leaving survivors who are aware of their hideout, and will pursue the party relentlessly.

## The Bound Magistrate
Another exception is the Bound Magistrate - anyone who reaches him is, by necessity, trespassing, and needs to be punished to the fullest extent of the law. 

# Layout

## Ground Level - Reception and Barracks (L1)
### 1.  Reception
A long hall into the center of the tower where a desk still sits. A low-level functionary would sit at this desk and direct visitors to the correct floor/area of the tower (or politely turn them away if they had no business there).  Two shafts cut through this area from higher in the tower down into the basement.

The spirit of the receptionist remains, and is still generally trying to be helpful. However, it has no idea the Tower of Smoke has collapsed, and response with laughs or confusion if anyone tries to convince it. "How absurd, I'd have heard that, I'm not deaf you know."

If a question or purpose can be framed as sensible given the spirits insistence everything is still as it was before the accident, it will happily explain where different things can be found, in this tower or the others.

### 2. Barracks
A chamber where the guard used to sleep and relax - in case the receptionists polite refusal was insufficient to keep someone out. The spirits here are more aggressive - no one who wasn't a guard was supposed to enter here, and they'll enforce that with violence quicker than most other spirits.

### 3. Prison
A jail cell with a few skeletons still present. The spirits that were here died when they tried to flee, destroyed by the guard outside.

### 4. Practice Area
When the guard needed to practice martial skills, this was the room they used.  

### 5. Public Library
The contents of these shelves were accessible to any citizen of Ashenspire, one of the only such places in this tower. A few quiet spirits still sit and read here, or peruse the stacks.

A door to the east leads to the rare books, and remains locked.

### 6. Public Library - Rare Books
The books and scrolls here were technically publicly available, but only under supervision or to trusted individuals.  These were considered either mildly dangerous, or, more often, fragile.

## Offices (L2)
While there is a fair amount of traffic here, the spirits here generally assume that if you made it past the receptionist they you're supposed to be here.  They may not take kindly to messing up their desks and papers though.

### 1. Stairwell
Mostly blocked off by the magical barriers.  Semi-heavy traffic at this level.

### 2. Maintenance Office
This office dealt with the maintenance of the towers and their workings underground. A ladder leads down a shaft into the basement.

### 3-8. Other Offices
Haven't yet determined the purpose of any of these offices - various minor functionaries, etc.

### 9. Cleaning Office
This office dealt with keeping the towers clean, inside and out. It was a fairly thankless division. A shaft leads through this room but does not open into it, going both up and down instead.

## Council Chambers (L4)

### 2. Council Room
This room was where the ruling council of Ashenspire would meet to discuss the running of the settlement. A glyph of truth is still active in the center of the room, where it forced any who came before the council to speak only true words, though it could not compel them to speak at all.

### 3. Council Member's Chambers
The council members lived on this floor as well, in rooms significantly more luxurious than those in the Tower of Cinders. One such chamber, on the far left, has a shaft cutting through it from above down to the floors below.

## Legal Quarter (L5)

### 2. Court Room
This is where those accused of committing crimes would stand before the magistrate and plead their case. Like the council chambers, a glyph of truth is still glowing on the floor here, before the magistrates massive stone desk.

### 3. Magistrate's Quarters
The magistrate used to live in these quarters. He still sometimes comes here, but generally the room is more often home to the Myconids who emerged here. The magistrate generally leaves them alone, his spirit assuming they are servants, here to prepare his rooms for him.

### 4. Other Quarters
The other, lesser functionaries who assisted the magistrate used to live here. For the most part their spirits are attending the Magistrate, and don't return often to their chambers.

## Ceremonial Chambers (L6)

### 2. Waiting Room
This room was used by those waiting for their turn in the ceremony room to the south east. A staircase here grants access to the roof, and it one of the only levels where the field has failed.

### 3. Ceremony Room
This room was used to perform ceremonies - weddings, etc, for Ashenspire. A glyph still glows in the center of the floor here, this one of binding - promises made while both participants stand in this circle cannot be easily broken.
