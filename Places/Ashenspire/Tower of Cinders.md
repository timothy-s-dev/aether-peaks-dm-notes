# Inhabitants
The spirits here are broken - fear, sorrow, and anger rule.  They are almost entirely unpredictable, as likely to flee the party weeping as they are to attack.  But they are also densely packed in places here, where even without ill intent they make the location dangerous.

## The Heartmother Shade
Once a community leader, the Heartmother died trying to lead others to safety.  She is still trying, and won't take kindly to any who she perceives as hindering that effort. Those that help, however, she may ally with, and help calm her fellow spirits to allow them passage.

# Layout
The Tower of Cinders is split into three primary sections.  The primary communal spaces are found on the first two floors, residential space is found on floors three and four, and floor five is artisans workshops, a market, and a medical space. The roof contains a now-overgrown garden and abandoned rookery, both of which have been largely taken over by a flock of [[Fog Skimmers]].

## Ground Level (L1)

### 1. Collapsed Entryway
The doors to the Tower of Cinder have collapsed inwards, with much of the stone above blocking the entryway.  Clearing it would be possible, but would take several days of hard labor to clear away the rubble, and there's a risk more of the tower would collapse if that "support" were removed.

### 2. Hearth Hall
A large communal space with a fire-pit in the center.  Once a primary gathering and dining space, but now home to the largest/densest swarm of spirits in [[Ashenspire]].

The undead wander mostly aimlessly here, and generally ignore the party. However, having one pass through you remains painful, regardless of the spirits intent/awareness. If a PC starts their turn in this room they must make an Agility or Intuition power roll to determine how well they are able to avoid the spirits. If they are distracted or unaware of the danger, decrease the tier of the result by one step.

* <11 - Take 8 corruption damage, P<2 weakened (save ends)
* 12-16 - Take 6 corruption damage, P<1 weakened (save ends)
* 17+ - Take 4 corruption damage, P<0 weakened (save ends)
### 3. Kitchens
A few ghosts here continue their daily tasks, cleaning or cooking, but this area sees relatively little traffic.

### 4. Pantry
The food here has long since rotted away.  Occasionally a spirit or two will wander in from the Hearth Hall (L1-2) or Kitchen (L1-3), but this area is generally fairly quiet. A trap-door on the wall closest to the tower entrance leads down into the basement (B-1)

### 5. Illicit Temple
While the only official religion for quite a while was the worship of the God Emperor, other religions never fully died out.  This room contains small altars to several unknown deities, and a few benches.  It is inaccessible from the first floor, but has a ladder leading up to a trap door in the God King's temple (L2-4) and the cave tunnels in the basement. The trap door in the God King's temple is under a statue, and cannot be opened from the bottom until that statue has been moved.

Ghosts don't generally wander in here from the Hearth Hall (L1-2), but occasionally float in through the floor or ceiling. Those that do are more prone to flight/fight responses than elsewhere in the tower, as they don't feel they're supposed to be there, and don't recognize the party as fellow worshipers.

## Second Story (L2)
This level sees significantly less spirit activity than the first floor, in part due to the efforts of the myconids in the overgrown baths (L2-5).
### 1. Landing
A mostly open space with stairs leading down to the Hearth Hall (L1-2) and up to the higher levels of the tower.  These are, as with other stairs, blocked by the magical fields.  The field covering the balcony overlooking the fire pit, however, has failed.  Dropping down from here may be ill-advised though, as the sheer number of spirits in the Hearth Hall (L1-2) make standing around there dangerous.

### 2. Storage
A large area set aside for storage, apparently mostly of tools and materials for making things.  A couple small work benches are here, but most things would have been taken up to the artisan workshops higher in the tower.

The myconids from the Overgrown Baths occasionally use the tools and materials found here, and sometimes exit through these windows; though they have to come back in via the ziplines in the Waterkeeper's Chambers (L2-3).

### 3. Waterkeeper's Chambers
The community leader of Ashenspire was based out of this room.  A large desk rests in the middle of the room, with shelves covered in books along one wall.  Two zip-lines lead out of this room.  One over to the second level of the [[Tower of Smoke]], and one to one of the reading rooms on the Public Records floor of the [[Tower of Ash]] (L3-4).

### 4. God Emperor's Temple
This quarter of the floor is set aside for the worship of the God Emperor.  A pulpit and pews are set up on one end, and a large, candle-covered alter just beyond the doors.  On the far side of the room from the pulpit is a large statue of the God Emperor - beneath which can be found a trap door leading down to the Illicit Temple (L1-5).

### 5. Overgrown Baths
A colony of myconids arose here after the rot from the spilled water of the baths was affected by the magical accident that destroyed the Tower of Smoke.

For the most part they prefer to avoid conflict with the spirits and the Fog Skimmers on the roof, though they are occasionally forced into conflict with both groups.  They generally stay in their chambers during the rising tide, emerging only after the Fog Skimmers have likely fallen asleep again.

## Third Story (L3)
This floor and the floor above are mostly bedrooms.  This floor is generally inaccessible unless the party manages to somehow reach the broken bridge (L3-2).
### 1. Stairwells and Halls
Each stairwell can be taken both up and down in a spiral, though both directions are blocked by magical barriers.  The halls here lead into the various bedrooms, as well as the ruined remains of a bridge that once led to the third level of the Tower of Smoke.

The stairs see some traffic, but the hallways are generally empty.

### 2. Broken Bridge
This room contains a door leading out to a collapsed bridge. Before the accident that bridge led across to the Tower of Smoke, allowing for easier access for those who worked in that tower but lived in this one.

Occasionally spirits float across as if the bridge were still there.

### 3. Bedrooms
The rooms here are large, but not richly appointed.  Each has plenty of space, if not a great deal of furniture or decorations.  Some may still have a few spirits in them, and those spirits basically never leave those rooms; mostly families whose last moments were together there, loath to part even now.

## Fourth Story (L4)
More bedrooms; these accessible only by dropping down from the floor above into a few of the western-most rooms.

### 1. Stairwells
Blocked by magical barriers, these are the only access points for this floor.  They see some spirit traffic, but not a ton.

### 2. Halls
These rooms provided access to the bedrooms, and are virtually always empty.

### 3. Bedrooms
Like the bedrooms on the floor below (L2-3), these rooms are large, but not richly appointed.  Some to the west are missing sections of their roof, granting access to them from the workshops above (L5-6 and L5-7).

### 4. Joined Rooms
The wall between these two bedrooms has been smashed down at some point in the past.

## Fifth Story (L5)

### 1. Stairwells
Blocked by magical barriers going up and down, sees minimal traffic from above, but some from below.

### 2. Hospital
This room was set up to help the sick and injured, and was busy on the day of the fall. Wards at the doorways are still active, and instantly clean anyone and anything that passes over them, to minimize infections.  The racks of medicine and potions on the wall are almost entirely depleted, but a couple Health Potions (DSH p. 316) remain.

The Heartmother Shade is often found here, as she was in life, trying to help in any way she can.

### 3. Market
Abandoned market stalls take up most of this room - the food and other perishables long since rotted away, though some tools and other goods remain.

### 4. Cartographer's Workshop
A lightly enchanted map of the Aether Peaks still rests on the table in this room; anyone looking at it has an immediate sense of where on it they are currently standing. It is a well made map, and can be taken by the party to grant an edge on rolls for navigating the wilds between the settlements of the Aether Peaks, given they have time to consult it.
