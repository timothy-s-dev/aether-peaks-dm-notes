#faction #stub 

The Claw is an organization within the Empire which handles espionage and assassinations. They are one of the only imperial organizations still managed directly by [[Empress Laseen]] herself. Two factors play heavily into that decision: she fears the organization could be misapplied in someone else's hands, and she fears the organization could be wielded against her (as she wielded it against the [[God Emperor]] in the [[The Night of Knives|Night of Knives]]).

For the most part, they are now primarily spies and information gatherers, though each member is still a highly trained killer if the need arises.
