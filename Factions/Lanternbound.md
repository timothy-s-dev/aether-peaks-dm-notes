#faction #stub 

The Lanternbound are a demonic cult with the emblem of an inverted lantern wrapped in chains. They believe that the Emperor wielded a secret, primordial magical power when he broke the world, and that the Roiling Fog was merely a way to conceal that power from everyone else.

> _"Light does not only fall from above; it rises from below"

They refer to this power as the "primordial glow", and believe that by communing with demons and gaining access to the world below the Roiling Fog, they too could wield that power.

Their goals include:

• Investigate cracks, breaches, weak points
	Anywhere the Fog surges or runes fail, they see opportunity.
	They aren’t trying to collapse settlements—but they do want to “study” the boundaries.
• Capture or commune with lesser demons
	They experiment with interrogation, coercion, and pact-binding rituals.
	They view demons as “luminaries” rather than threats.
• Gather forbidden texts and relics of the old wards
	Especially materials connected to the God Emperor’s magics.
	They want to “reverse-engineer” the barriers.
• Create vessels for carrying Fog-energy safely
	Crystal lanterns, smoldering braziers, etched brass cages.
	Most of these devices leak corruption.

In secret, the cult is being manipulated heavily by the Talon, who see them as one tool for making Laseen look weak, so they can pitch the return of the God Emperor as a good thing.

While they think they're getting the better of the demons, they're often being manipulated from that side as well.  They are given false "visions" of the power they seek, both below the fog and wielded by the emperor, granted small boons with tricks to them (fog resistance that still corrupts them, just doesn't immediately kill them, false insights into ancient runes), and the demons manipulate the fog for them, allowing the cultists to think they have control over it themselves.
