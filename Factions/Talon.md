The Talon is a secretive group of assassins working to resurrect [[God Emperor|The God Emperor]] and return him to power.
## History
The Talon arose on[[The Night of Knives]]; when [[Claw|The Claw]] turned on the [[God Emperor]] there were some who balked. Most of the Emperor's allies were eliminated in the purge, but a few escaped, including ex-members of the Claw. Most of these opted to disappear as best as they were able, but a few split off to form the Talon.
