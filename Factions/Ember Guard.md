#faction #stub 

The Ember Guard is a small, elite order of heroes, tasked with traveling the Peaks to defend the people of the Empire, and preserve the fragile unity of the highlands.

They were originally a special forces unit tasked with fighting back the demonic forces, and that is still technically part of their job. They are rarely called on to do so now, but the emblems they wear can be used to summon them to that purpose if and when the need arises.
