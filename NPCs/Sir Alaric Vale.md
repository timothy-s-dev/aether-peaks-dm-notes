#npc

Sir Alaric Vale is a member of the [[Ember Guard]], tall and lean, built like a duelist rather than a soldier. His formal coat — deep ember red trimmed in black thread — is cut perfectly to his frame, and the brass clasps gleam like captured sunlight. At his throat rests the sigil of the Ember Guard, worn smooth with age.

His hair is close-cropped and dark, showing the faintest silver at the temples. His eyes are pale — gray, almost colorless — and when they meet yours, there’s a steadiness that feels sort of appraising, as though constantly taking your measure. His smile comes easily, but never quite reaches those eyes.

Sir Alaric Vale carries himself with the composure of a man long accustomed to ceremony — and to being watched. His every movement is deliberate: the bow of the head that lingers a heartbeat too long, a stillness between gestures as though he’s pausing to make sure it was noticed.

He is a member of [[Ember Guard]], and well respected enough to be tasked with delivering letters to Aspirants.

%% Secret
The man the party knows as Sir Alaric Vale is actually a member of the Talons, a group of assassins that broke away from Empress Laseen's Claw after she turned on the Emperor, and who pursue his return (and her death). His real name is Saren Kael.

Saren Kael assassinated the real Sir Alaric Vale between Stormcradle and Dimdale. He looks almost nothing like the real Sir Alaric did (see image below). While the real Sir Alaric _was_ born in Ravenroost, his family moved to Stormcradle when he was a child, and he had only traveled any further east than that once on a mission for the Ember Guard (a fact Saren was banking on to avoid someone recognizing the name but not his face).
