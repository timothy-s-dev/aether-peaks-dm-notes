#npc 

The God Emperor has been dead for over a thousand years, but portraits remain that portray him as a tall, strong, bearded man in his prime. Frequently in a huge suit of armor with his legendary blade, Ashlight, on his back or in his hand.
## Traits and Motivations
Little is known about the God Emperor's origins; the only other known immortals all died with him on the Night of Knives, except for now-Empress Laseen, who doesn't seem inclined to reminisce.

## History
He came to power after uniting the mortal kin in the war against the demons; immediately following his victory his rule is rumored to have been relatively fair a just. But as time went on his grip grew tighter, and his rule cruel.

Eventually Laseen, his master of assassins couldn't stand by any longer and turned on him. He an his closest allies and advisors were killed by Laseen's order of assassins, The Claw in a single night, now referred to as the Night of Knives.
