#npc

Laseen has short-cropped black hair and a scar on her left cheek. She is rarely seen without her official Empress' garb, which consists of a white tabbard stitched with gold, a black-and-gold coif and pauldron, and a red shoulder-cape.
## Traits and Motivations
Laseen is distant, and generally prefers to rule through intermediaries; trusted advisors and organizations. Little is known of her personality to any but a select few.

What can be determined from her style of rule is a distinct desire to avoid the iron-fist approach of the late Emperor.

%% Secret
Laseen has long since lost any semblance of a moral compass - a side effect, she believes, of the immortality she and the Emperor shared. Unlike the Emperor, when this started happening to her, she was deeply disturbed by it, and has managed to hold on to that through the centuries.

As a result, she prefers not to trust her own judgement when the lives and well-being of others are at stake (which is often, in her line of work). She instead tries her best to surround herself with people and organizations she can trust to fight for the right thing, and guide her to it. (Think Amos Burton)

Generally this takes the form of her Advisory Council, which consists of a few direct trusted advisors, as well as the heads of most of the government bodies under her. The one notable exception is the Claw, who have no representation on the Council, and answer directly to Laseen. In part because she doesn't think that's an organization that can be "good" for the people of the empire, and in part because she fears it being used against her, as she used it against the Emperor in the Night of Knives.

## Routine
Empress Laseen spends most of her days in meetings and audiences, answering questions and resolving conflicts between the various instruments of state.
