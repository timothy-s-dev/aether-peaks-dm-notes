For this campaign I want to explore the idea of the contrasting strength and weakness inherent in relying on others.

This is visible in individual characters (Laseen relies on her advisors and the Ember Guard as a sort of moral compass) as well as larger structures (the various settlements are heavily reliant on one another for their continued survival).

From the PCs perspective, I want to try, as much as possible, to encourage them to rely on both certain NPCs, as well as each other.  I don't think I want to make betrayal a major component/theme, though deception isn't absent in the slightest (see [[Sir Alaric Vale]]).
