Standard encounter (EV 18-24 for 3 Heroes, 24-30 for 4, and 30-36 for 5)

The party has found the entrance to the cultists' hideout, but it's not undefended.  Before they can get inside they'll have to deal with the guards out front.

If they kill, rout, or otherwise bypass the guards the party gets 1 victory.

For 3 Heroes (EV 20)
* Group 1 (EV 11)
	* 1x Human Death Cultist (EV 8)
	* 4x Human Death Acolyte (EV 3)
* Group 2 (EV 9)
	* 1x Human Scoundrel (EV 6)
	* 4x Human Guard (EV 3)

For 4 Heroes, double the Acolytes and Guards (+6 EV to EV 26)

For 5 Heroes, **don't** double the Acolytes and Guards, and instead add a third group:
* Group 3 (EV 11)
	* 1x Human Death Cultist (EV 8)
	* 4x Ensnarer (EV 3)
For a total of EV 31
