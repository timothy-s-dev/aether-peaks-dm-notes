Hard encounter (EV 24-30 for 3 Heroes, 30-36 for 4, and 36-42 for 5)

The party has worked their way to the center of the cultists' stronghold, and now they have to deal with its leader and his bound demon.  The leader has no desire to die, and will flee if his health drops below half after ordering the demon (and his remaining acolytes) to kill the party. If he is killed the demon is freed from its bindings; this doesn't stop it trying to kill the party, but it (and the other demons) also turn on the remaining acolytes.

Killing or routing the cultists and destroying the demons grants the party 2 victories. If the cult leader and head demon both escape they earn only 1 victory.

For 3 Heroes (EV 28)
* Group 1 (EV 11)
	* 1x Human Death Cultist (EV 8)
	* 4x Human Death Acolyte (EV 3)
* Group 2 (EV 11)
	* 1x Muceron (EV 5)
	*  8x Frenzied (EV 6)
* Group 3 (EV 6)
	* 8x Human Death Acolyte

For 4 Heroes, replace groups 2 and 3 with a Chorogaunt (Total EV 31)

For 5 Heroes, replace just group 2 with a Chorogaunt (Total EV 37)
