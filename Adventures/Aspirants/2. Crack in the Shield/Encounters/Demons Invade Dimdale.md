Demons have flooded out from the mist and attacked an outlying farm; people are trapped in the various structures and need to be rescued.

The demons are split up by group and each is trying to break into a different structure.  Once inside they will try to find and kill everyone.

If the party manage to kill all the demons and save at least half the townspeople they earn a victory.  If they manage to kill all the demons and save everyone they earn a victory and the Local Hero title (for all PCs who participated in the defense).

There are three groups of demons. For 3 PCs each group consists of:
* 4x Ensnarer
* 4x Frenzied

For 4 PCs each group consists of
* 1x Muceron
* 4x Ensnarer **OR** 4x Frenzied

For 5 PCs the groups are the same as for 4 PCs, but there is a 4th group with just a Muceron that works with the group attacking the main structure.
