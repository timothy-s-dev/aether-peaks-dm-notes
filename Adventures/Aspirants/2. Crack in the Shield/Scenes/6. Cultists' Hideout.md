# Overview
The cultists have found a cave in one of the mountains to the north east of Dimdale, up on the mountainside overlooking the town.  They have set up a camp there where they're staying between attempts at sabotage.

One group is staying outside, [[Cultists' Hideout Entrance|keeping watch]].  The leader and the remainder of the group are [[Cult Leader|inside]].  A conflict outside gives the cultists inside time to set up defenses, and guarantees they aren't taken by surprise.

The cult leader, Maelor Vesk (Seer Vesk, to his underlings), isn't particularly interested in talking - he's confident his demonic ally(s) can deal with whatever rabble Dimdale can throw at them.  If the party comes up with a clever way to get him to talk, he has the the Power and Discovery Motivations, and the Justice and Peace Pitfalls.

If the party kills the cultists outside (or the potential ambushers) he's not terribly inclined to believe anything they say without very, very good reasons.  He'll have positioned himself to be imposing, relying on his presence (and that of his demon "buddy") to "chase off this chaff."
