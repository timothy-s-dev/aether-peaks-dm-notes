# Overview
The party may opt to visit area the demons attacked.  There's not a great deal here for them to discover, but there are some subtle hints they might pick up on:

* **Medium Lore (Magic/Religion) Check**
	* <=11 - You can tell the Fog was here, but mostly because of all the dead goats.
	* 12-16 - The plant life here looks slightly warped but not fully corrupted - the Roiling Fog was here, but only very briefly, someone pushed it back.
	* 17+ - As 12-16, plus some of the nearby plants have been dug up, or had leaves/berries harvested.
* **Medium Interpersonal (Handle Animals, required) Check**
	* <=11 - It's a bunch of dead goats.  Probably the demons' work.
	* 12-16 - The goats were all clumped together when they were attacked, it looks like the demons surrounded them before they had a chance to bolt. (implies the demons left the Fog here too, which is, again, very unusual)
	* 17+ - One of the goats was removed from the pile (On a 17+ on the track check below, they can determine that it was taken by the group that left to the NE)
* **Easy Intrigue (Search/Track) Check** 
	* <=11 - There are a fair number of footprints here, but a few stick out - a large group leads off to the North East (Cultists), a smaller set including one set of heavy boots (Bram's) go back and forth to town.
	* 12-16 - As 11, plus one, smaller, lighter pair that lead off towards the Western edge of town (Elana's, she popped in in the dead of morning before Bram got there to see if there was anything to learn.)
	* On a 17+ they can choose to follow any one set of tracks, but know that the others are likely to have faded by the time they return.

## Overnight Watch
The party may also opt to watch the wall over-night to see if the saboteur returns.  They don't come to quite the same place (warned by Elara, unless the party keeps her busy all day), so if they don't spread out or patrol they'll miss the cultists returning.

If they do keep watch (either patrolling or spread out along the wall), they can make a **Medium Intrigue (Alertness) Group Test** to determine if they spot them.
* <=11 - They are noticed by the cultists and [[Ambushed by Cultists!|ambushed]] by some of them to keep the party busy while they sabotage a second area of the wall.
* 12-16 - They notice the cultists at the same time as the cultists notice them.
* 17+ - They notice the cultists and have the opportunity to surprise them.

If they make the 17+ and choose to attack, the cultists split, with some still trying to complete the damage while the others hold off the party.  None of them want to die, and will flee if reduced low enough.  One group of four minions splits off to do the damage while the rest engage the party.  They don't clump up - one works on the wall while the other stand a short distance from them in a defensive position.  If one falls or flees, another steps in to take their place doing the damage. 

If they have 2 full rounds of damaging the wall (3 if they were surprised), they get a small breach, and one more turn widens it before they turn and fight.  If another round starts after they get the breach open, a group of 4-8 (depending on party size) Ensnarers burst through the wall, and attack the party.

If the entire group is killed, the cultists will flee after round 3 or 4, depending on how they're doing.
