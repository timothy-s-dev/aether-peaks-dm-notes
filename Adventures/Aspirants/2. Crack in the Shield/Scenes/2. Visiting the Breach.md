# Overview
> A scaffold of rough planks and ladders leans against the breach, a man-tall section of stone spider-webbed with fractures and blackened scorch marks, runes guttering and dim. A short distance from the wall a cluster of townsfolk watch, as a half‑dozen workers in leather aprons and dust‑covered cloaks move with weary urgency, hauling mortar, chisels, and coils of copper wire.
> 
> At their center stands a broad‑shouldered, dark-skinned dwarf with a (relatively) short, black beard and a leather tool‑belt heavy with chisels and vials. Sweat and stone dust streak his forearms; a faint blue glow clings to the mallet in his hand.
> 
> *“Work zone’s closed,”* he calls, voice like gravel over steel. *“If you’re here to gawk, join the crowd and stay out of our way.”*

# Key Discoveries
* FREEBIE - The damage was definitely deliberate sabotage. Garrick and his crew had repaired some minor marring of the wards in this exact spot yesterday. Today the repairs were undone and there was additional damage in the same spot.
* Easy Magic Check - The damage was precise - whoever performed the sabotage has either a solid understanding of warding, or detailed instructions from someone who does.
* Difficult Track Check - There are a great many footprints leading off towards the cultists' hideout to the North-East.

If the party offers to help, or wants to look, Garrick introduces himself: *"Name’s Garrick Stoneveil. Foreman of this mess."*

Garrick is okay with the party looking, but not touching, the damage.  He watches them like a hawk, still not 100% convinced it wasn't them, given the timing.  *“If you know your sigils, I won’t say no to a second opinion. Just don’t take offense if I argue with you. It’s my wall on the line. You spot something, you point - don't touch a thing.”*

If they ask him about the nature of the damage, he tells them straight up that it was sabotage: *"We were out here just yesterday, smoothing over a bit of ward scuffing. Nothing major. Today? It’s like someone took a chisel to my work and then kicked it for good measure. Stone doesn’t crack this way on its own."*

If they notice and point out the precice nature of the damage, he concurs: *"Aye, I think you're right - these runes don’t fail in the middle like that. You cut in the wrong place, nothing happens. Cut in the right place, and the whole line sags. This… this was the right place. Someone knew what they were doing, or had a damned good set of instructions."*

If they find and point out the tracks, he's apologetic for confusing the tracks: *"Ah, darkness take me, now that you point them out I see them. Wish I'd noticed them on the way in, could have avoided confusing them so badly."*

If they ask about the repairs: *"I'm hopeful we'll be able to get it fully patched by this evening, but I'll be warning folk to keep away from the wall and keeping a Torch-Guard on it just in case. But if this happens again somewhere else along the wall we could be in big trouble.  Damage like this takes longer to fix than it does to cause...."*

He refuses to speculate on who the saboteur might be - he doesn't think it's likely anyone who lives in Dimdale, but won't commit 100% to that either. If they find the tracks he sees this as confirmation that it was a larger group of outsiders, probably camping out somewhere up on the mountain-sides overlooking the town.
