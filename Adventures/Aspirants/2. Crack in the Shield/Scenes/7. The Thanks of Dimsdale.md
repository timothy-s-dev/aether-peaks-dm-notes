# Overview
The heroes have (probably) saved Dimdale from a widening breach, a demon incursion, and the Lanternbound cell meddling outside town. This scene is a quiet comedown: townsfolk reactions, a chance to take stock, and a formal send‑off on their pilgrimage.

If the party saved most people in “Demons Invade Dimdale” and stopped further breaches, word has spread. If they failed publicly (botched mob negotiation, killed townsfolk, allowed a big incursion), the tone should lean far more more “shell‑shocked village” than “resilient hometown.”

# Key NPC Reactions

### Waterkeeper Tobias
* Tobias is openly grateful and encouraging if things went reasonably well.
* If the party at least tried he is kind about it, but if they were violent or appeared to make things worse he's cold if courteous, and clearly not sorry to see their backs.
* If the party talked Elara into coming clean, he's very grateful:
	* _“You found rot in our own roots and didn’t just chop down the tree,”_ Tobias says, voice low. _“Whatever happens next with Elara, you gave us a chance to deal with this as neighbors, not executioners. And for that I am very grateful.”_

### Garrick Stoneveil
* If the party was instrumental in saving the town, Garrick is unusually open with his praise:
	* _“Wall’s holding,”_ he reports, as if answering an unspoken question. _“Repairs wouldn’t have gone half so fast if you hadn’t bought us the time. Dimdale is here because you stood with us"._
* If they helped but caused chaos:
	* _You lot fight like a landslide—loud, and messy, but somehow the wall’s still standing at the end of it. And I suppose that's what counts.”_
* If they were problematic, Garrick doesn't come to see them off at all.

### Silas Croft
* If the group ended on a positive note he is present, but probably doesn't say much.
* If they owe him a favor, he reminds them of it before they leave:
	* *Well, you've certainly made an impression, something I know a bit about.* He gestures at his outfit. _“Try not to get yourselves killed before we can settle our accounts properly, hmm?”_

### Elara Vance
* If Elara died, someone may comment on it; *"To think, one of our own... she knew what that wall protects us from.  Still, I wish... well, doesn't much matter now.*
* If she survived but escaped, someone may comment on it: *"If she's alive out there, it's because you drove her off before she could do worse. Hopefully she uses the opportunity to re-evaluate some things... but I wouldn't count on it."*
* If she was talked into turning herself in, she herself is there (under guard) and tries to catch the party before they leave: *"I suppose I should thank you; not may people would have tracked me down just to argue to keep me around. Whatever Tobias decides, I'll try to make this right."*
	* In this event she also gives the party a recipe for the Portable Cloud she may have tried using, as well as a bottle of the fey-grove rain-water required to make one.

### Vinn Berghish
* If the party approached her about the sabotage at any point after arriving in Dimdale, she'll follow up:
	* *"I sent my report to the Cablewrights' Guild; they agree with my assessment, that line was cut, and deliberately. The investigators are still on their way, but I'll try to get word to you, once we find out who did this."*

# Rewards
* If the party was instrumental in saving Dimdale and generally avoided causing major trouble, even Bram will be present; *"We got off on the wrong foot. What you did here, for me, and the rest of Dimdale... well, we won't forget it, and we won't let anyone else either."
	* The party gains 1 Renown

# Farewell
> Tobias approaches the party once more, right before they step away: *"Your pilgrimage doesn't end here.  Stormcradle's next, and whatever waits for you there. But if your path ever brings you back this way, know you'll find a welcome in Dimdale."
>
> *"Go, with clear skies - may your journey to Stormcradle be a much duller one than that which brought you to us!"*
