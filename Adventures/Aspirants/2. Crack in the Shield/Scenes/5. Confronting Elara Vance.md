# Overview
While Elara Vance *was* definitely involved, she's not so dumb as to fold the instant the party walks through her door.  They'll need to gather evidence first, and there are a few potential sources for that evidence:
* They may notice while [[3. Scene of the Crime|visiting the scene of the crime]] that some of the plants were harvested, and/or follow her tracks back to her home.
	* If they do this and sneak into her home while she's out and about, they may be able to find the plants taken from the scene of the crime, and use those as evidence as well.
* They may speak [[4. Meeting with Silas Croft|speak with Silas]], and connect the dots that she was the one he overheard.
* They may capture one of the cultists, perhaps while [[3. Scene of the Crime|watching the wall at night]], after being [[Ambushed by Cultists!||ambushed]], or even after hunting down the cultists [[6. Cultists' Hideout|in their lair]].


# No Evidence
If they are accusatory with no evidence she brushes them off as clearly insane, and offers them a tea that she says will help restore their wits, since they've clearly had more drink than was good for them.

If they threaten violence she will pretend to shirk back, but grab and throw a fistful of something on the ground from a pouch at her side.  The room fills with a dense smoke, and she tries to duck backwards out a hidden door in the rear.  Attempts to find and catch her in the fog are a **Hard Intrigue Test** made with a double-bane - the fog is filled with a pollen that messes with your sense of direction, and the room is full of dangerous alchemical stuff.
* <=11 - You stumble into a basket of something and it *burns*; take 8 points of damage.
* 12-16 - You fail to catch Elara, and  bash your shin into the furniture for your trouble.
* 17+ - You a hand on Elara, preventing her from escaping.

If caught she'll fall back on assuming the law will be on her side.  She can't stop them from killing her, but if they don't, they've just pretty heavily incriminated themselves, attacking a local.
# Negotiating
Convincing Elara to come clean will be no easy task.  She is Neutral by default (2 Interest, 3 Patience), though that may be modified by how well the opening negotiation with the mob went (+1 Interest on 4/5 Interest, -1 Patience on <=2 Interest)

**Motivations**
* Discovery - she wants to know if she can use the fog to improve her alchemy (she knows they do this in [[Fogreach]], so it can't be *that* dangerous, right?)
* Legacy - she wants to discover something so big no one will ever forget her name

**Pitfalls**
* Greed - she's not in it for the money - she just wants recognition, not wealth.
* Justice - not in a broad sense, but in this case, she'd really prefer "justice" was a thing that didn't happen to her, specifically. In fact, she'd really like her part in all this kept very, very quiet.

She is, for what it's worth, apologetic if caught - she never wanted anyone to get hurt; even Bram's stupid goats (well, maybe the goats, they kept getting into her garden).  But think of the opportunities for breakthroughs!  The Fog has been shown to confer alchemical properties, and the wards alter it, who knows what it could be used for!

Under no circumstances will she agree to out herself in front of the whole town, but given a high enough degree of success she might agree to talk to Tobias, if the PCs will vouch for her.

If convinced, she can give the exact location of the cultists hideout, and potentially even help draw them into a trap.

She won't admit to still having the plants she took unless the party found them, or they roll exceptionally well; she'll claim the corruption destroyed them before she could make use of them.
