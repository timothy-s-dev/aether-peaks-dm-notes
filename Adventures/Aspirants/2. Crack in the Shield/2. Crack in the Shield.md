# Overview
A small cell of [[Lanternbound]] cultists have set up camp a short ways outside Dimdale. They have sabotaged the wards on the barrier wall, in the hopes of studying the Roiling Fog that leaks through (particularly the effect the wards have on the Fog).

In town, [[Waterkeeper Tobias]] and [[Garrick Stoneveil]] are working furiously to repair the wall before further damage can take place, and find out how it got damaged in the first place.  [[Elara Vance]], a local herbalist, is secretly working with the cultists in the hopes of gaining membership, and access to their arcane secrets.  [[Silas Croft]], a local merchant, has recently become aware of the plot, but has yet to decide if he should interfere - it could be good for business, having the Ember Guard make the trip out.

There are seven (7) potential victory points in this adventure, as planned.

## Their Own Betrayal
While in town the party may try to look for information about what happened to their cable-car.  Unfortunately for them, [[Sir Alaric Vale|the guilty party]] isn't here, and there aren't a lot of leads.  If they seek out [[Vinn Berghish]], she'll let them know that she has notified her boss at the Cablewrights' Guild, and there are investigators on the way, but they won't arrive for about a week.

It would be a logical conclusion to assume that the saboteurs of the Dimdale shield wall are the same as those who caused the cable-car collapse, and Vinn may even ask or encourage them to chase that lead, but it wasn't the cultists.

Their first real hint will come in Stormcradle, where they may be able to discover that Sir Alaric Vale, wasn't really Sir Alaric Vale.
