#npc 

> *"The wards are failing and the Fog is coming; the question isn't one of survival, Dimdale is tough, but who will come out on top when the dust settles... that remains to be seen."*

An older Polder gentleman who works as a merchant in [[Places/Dimdale]]. He dresses in garishly bright and colorful, fancy clothes, and is frequently seen fiddling with a gold coin. He frequently gestures with an upraised finger - both to provide emphasis, and to shake at "young troublemakers."  He is pragmatic to a fault, and does little without first confirming it will benefit him, and beyond that, benefit him more than anyone else.

Silas overheard a conversation in The Weeping Kettle between [[Elara Vance]] and one of the [[Lanternbound]] cultists, though he didn't risk a peek at the table to identify either individual.  What he *does* know, is that the Cultists are responsible for weakening the barrier, and that a local woman is helping them.  He hasn't brought this information to [[Waterkeeper Tobias]] just yet - he's still trying to figure out how he can benefit most from the situation.
