> _“We don’t own the water. We borrow it, same as breath. When we forget that, the mountains remind us.”_

A human man in his late fifties, lean and weathered, with pale eyes and a thin braid of silver hair down one shoulder. He wears dark blue robes belted with copper rings. His hands are stained with chalk from marking ration ledgers.

## Traits and Motivations
He speaks with a quiet authority and deliberate calm, like someone used to coaxing people through bad news without spreading panic.

## Routine
Tobias' primary job involves making sure everyone gets enough to drink, and enough water is set aside for the less rainy seasons. However, his is also a position of power in the town, and so he is also frequently called on to help settle disputes, and weigh in on solutions for problems facing the town.
