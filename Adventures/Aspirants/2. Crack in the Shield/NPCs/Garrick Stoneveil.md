#npc

> _“Everyone thinks strength means not breaking. Truth is, it’s knowing how to set yourself right after you do.”_

A broad-shouldered, dark-skinned dwarf, his (relatively) short black beard is bound with copper rings, and his heavy gloves are streaked with mud and ash. His voice booms when he wants it to, but more often speaks in a quieter, deep, gravelly tone.

## Traits and Motivations
Garrick is generally quiet and reserved when not working. He gets enough of bossing folks around on the job, and doesn't want to deal with it the rest of the time. Most of his after-work interactions are with his co-workers, after his family passed in a Fog breach a few decades back.

## Routine
Garricks day-to-day involves walking the perimeter of the ridges and protective walls around Dimdale; he looks for flaws, immediate or potential, and oversees the repairs necessary to keep the town safe.
