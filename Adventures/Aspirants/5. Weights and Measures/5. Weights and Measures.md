#stub 

Highspire is even steeper than most places the Empire has built its settlements, and so relies heavily on a system of lifts and chutes for transporting people and goods.

These lifts and the mechanisms that drive them are maintained by mostly underpaid, lower-class workers. One of the main cargo lifts is in a sorry state, and the workers are fed up and are on strike, refusing to fix it until demands are met.
# Potential Outline
1. Arrival
	* Party gets to the city and sees how much it relies on the lifts, and are told they'll have to take the ladder, as the lift they need is currently too dangerous to operate.
2. The Problem
	* The party discovers that the lift is offline due to a strike by the workers who maintain it. The city official in charge of the lifts would like the party to act as mediators, since they're outsiders who don't have a side yet.
3. The Holdouts
	* A negotiation takes place with the workers; they want better wages, a safer environment (mostly better equipment), and are concerned about retaliation.  The city wants to save as much money as possible, but get the lift running again soon.  There is a risk they'll run it without the necessary maintenance if things carry on too long, risking the lives of the workers who load/unload it, as well as those who live below it.
4. Complication
	* Mid negotiation someone sabotages the machinery - whose "side" did it?  Do they take credit, or try to frame the other side? Was it an outside force? (Seems less likely)
	* Party (hopefully) helps rescue the people put at risk, and maybe prevent permanent damage to the lift.
5. Aftermath
	* Party should earn a reward dependent on which side they helped, or a different one if they somehow managed to find middle ground.
