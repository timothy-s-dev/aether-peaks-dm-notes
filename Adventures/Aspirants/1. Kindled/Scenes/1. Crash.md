We open on the party riding a cable-car across the valley between Stonehollow and Dimdale, the Roiling Fog visible beneath them.  They set off a day or two back, hiking across the mountains west of Stonehollow towards Dimdale, letters in hand, as Aspirants to the Ember Guard.  They boarded the cable-car earlier this evening, planning on resting at the wayhouse on the far side for the coming night.

> There's a sudden *-SNAP-*.  Not the groan of stressed metal, but the sharp, gut-deep crack of something vital giving way.
> 
> The floor tilts. Someone's gear slides across the planks as the world pitches backwards. For a heartbeat, you're weightless, and then the cable-car begins to fall.
> 
> Fragments of ice fly off the falling car and whip past the narrow openings, the wind roaring louder than thought.  You have less than a minute before the impact: What do you do?

## The Cable Car
### Layout
* The interior is about 10x6 feet, and about 7 feet tall. Enough for 4-6 passengers and luggage. 
* The exterior is made of wood banded with iron. 
* A sliding door on either short side can be used to access the interior, and benches run along the two long sides to provide seating. 
* Cargo netting and hooks line the walls and ceiling. 
* A small hatch in the ceiling can be used to climb on top and access the line/pulley mechanisms. 
* A hand-crank and brake-lever are situated in one corner, though both are intended for stablization and docking, not high speed/emergency use. 

### Functionality
* The car is attached beneath the main iron tension cable, riding a mechanical trolley with two wheels and a powered grip system. 
* It’s drawn by arcane-tethered winches from each end station — synchronized through bound crystals that keep tension steady between peaks. 
* A faint hum or low vibration runs through the floor when it’s in motion — the sound of distant winches, amplified by the cable. 
* Safety enchantments are minimal: runes to repel lightning and stabilize temperature, but nothing that could slow a catastrophic failure. Such are too rare, and often un-survivable regardless.

### Maintenance/Safety
* Every few weeks, engineers run maintenance cars along the lines — open-frame platforms with inspection tools, grease, and arcane diagnostic lenses.
* Routine passengers often tie harness lines to the car’s inner hooks — mostly habit, but sometimes lifesaving.
* The Stonehollow–Dimdale route is considered old but reliable… until now.
* Most locals have ridden one at least once; they respect them the way sailors respect old ships — with affection and a hint of superstition.

## The Initial Jolt
All PCs need to make a medium Might or Agility power roll (or an appropriate skill) to see how badly they get knocked around:
* <=11 - You are thrown across the compartment, slamming into the far wall and losing a piece of gear. What do you drop?
* 12-16 - You are knocked down, take 3 damage
* 17+ - You slight roughly to the side (which is now the bottom) of the car, but manage to avoid harm.

## What do you want to do?
The car isn't in a total free-fall, it's grinding backwards down the cable and swinging towards the far mountain-side.  The pace is terrifying, but not instantaneous death.  The party has maybe 30s to try to slow the car or otherwise survive as best as they can.  The fog is approaching fast, and while the impact might kill you, the things in the fog definitely will if the car falls too far.

* Climb to the roof or cable, though the car is swinging and the air is freezing thin. 
* Brace or anchor the car to the line with climbing gear, ropes, spikes, or magic. 
* Slow the fall through creative use of abilities. 
* Aid a companion if someone was injured in the initial snap. 
* Try to reach the brake on the far (now “top”) side of the car and slow the car a bit (can’t stop it).

The party will take 6 damage each from the crash - each successful action will reduce that by 2.
