The goal for this scene is just to get to know the remaining characters.  If they launch into something on their own, great, otherwise here are some questions to prompt with:

* What part of the celebration do you find yourself drawn to first — the food, the music, the crowd, or something quieter?
* How do people greet you as you move through the square? With respect? Teasing familiarity? Quiet awe?
* Is there someone here who seems proudest of you — or someone who still doesn’t think you should have been chosen?
* Is there someone you think should have been chosen instead of you? Do you seek them out or avoid them at the party?
* As the celebration swells around you, what emotion keeps slipping through the pride — fear, guilt, excitement, disbelief?
* What shared memory do you and another aspirant recall tonight — something that makes you laugh, or something unspoken that passes in silence?
* When you look out west, towards distance Emberglass Peak, what do you imagine waits for you there?
* Who do you feel a need to say goodbye to? How do they react to that farewell?

The party then has an opportunity to question Sir Alaric Vale about their upcoming journey.  He answers questions fairly clearly and openly.

## About the Ember Guard
* Originally formed by the Emperor to fight demons, now serve the Empress as messengers, problem solvers, and itinerant soldiers.
	* Often called on to solve problems across the empire when locals and/or existing infrastructure proves insufficient.
	* Still occasionally called on to aid in repelling demonic forces, but this is rare now.
* Ranks are small, 100-200 in the Empire at a time.
* The symbol of the Ember Guard - the brass sigil on Sir Alaric Vale's breast, is centuries old. Each member bears one passed down from another who fell in service to the Empire
	* "You'll learn of those who bore yours before you, should you reach Emberhold."

Answers about the Ember Guard feel rehearsed, if the party bothers to check him on any of it.  But nothing they can put their fingers on.

## About Himself
* Been with the guard for 20 years
* Originally from Ravenroost, took this job because it puts him close enough for a visit.
* Deflects questions about who bore his sigil before him - supposedly because it's a private matter (but in truth he doesn't know).

## The Journey Ahead
* The path is long - they should expect it to take several weeks at best.
	* Those who start from closer are often given a task further away to complete before turning towards Emberglass Peak.
* The party is expected to make use of whatever paths and travel methods they like.
* He suggests they not rush - the journey is long, and they should pace themselves.  There is an old watch-station at the far side of the cable-car run.
* Hardest stretch is probabl from Dimdale to Stormcradle - or at least, that's the farthest without a real bed to sleep in!  
* Don't dawdle in Ashenspire - don't even go in if you don't have to.

If the party watches him closely, his behavior is mostly normal.  He refuses all alcoholic drinks, and seems to tense up a bit when asked questions about the Emperor/Empress or his history in the Ember Guard.
