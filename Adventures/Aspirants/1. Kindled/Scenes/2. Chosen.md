# Character Introductions
Look to the first character, and ask the following questions:
* Where are you, and what are you doing, when this morning begins?
* What kind of work or duty usually fills your time here in Stonehollow?
* The messenger arrived yesterday.  People have been whispering.  What's the mood in the city right now?

If they seem interested, do a little role-play with whatever is going on around them; give them an opportunity to spotlight their character before the messenger arrives.  

# The Messenger

> There’s a pause — he examines your face a moment, as if to confirm something. Then the envelope appears, the red wax bright against worn leather.

> “Stonehollow has made its choice,” he says, with a small smile. “Congratulations, aspirant.”

The messenger introduces himself as Sir Alaric Vale, a middle-aged human man, tall and lean.  Built like a duelist, rather than a soldier. His formal coat - deep ember red trimmed in black thread - is cut perfectly to his frame.  At his breast rests the talisman of the Ember Guard, worn smooth with age and frequent contact.  His hair is close-cropped and dark, showing the faintest touch of silver at the temples.  His eyes are a pale-gray, and seem to be appraising you.

Ask the player the following questions:
* Do you open it immediately, or wait until you're alone?
* The letter names you as an Aspirant to the Ember Guard - what's your first thought?
* Who or what will you be leaving behind when you go?
