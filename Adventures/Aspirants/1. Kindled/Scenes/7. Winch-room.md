> The slope evens out at last, giving way to a narrow plateau carved into the mountainside. Ahead, three stone pillars rise to support a simple pavilion — the upper terminal of the cable line. Its roof groans in the wind, wooden shingles creaking, and the boarding platform juts over the drop like a tongue of weathered planks. 
> 
> The cable itself hangs slack over the chasm, its far end vanishing into the gray distance below. The faint smell of ozone still clings to the air, sharper here, like burned copper. 
> 
> Beside the pavilion stands a squat stone house with heavy shutters and a tall chimney: the cablewright’s quarters, attached to the winch room. The great drum and gears within are still and silent. 
> 
> To the west, a narrow footpath winds along the ridgeline — a pale thread leading toward distant Dimdale, a small line of smoke from fires rising into the sky. For the first time since the crash, the wind carries no trace of the Fog’s hiss below. Only the soft, endless murmur of the peaks.

If the party chooses to investigate the winch/mechanism they can discover: 
* The winch appears to be in perfect working order and mostly undamaged (some minor damage can safely be attributed to the sudden release of the cable from the far side).
	* Vinn will confirm this if she’s will the party - the problem was definitely either on the far side, or somewhere along the cable itself. 
* If Vinn is with them, she wants to pop into her home to report the cable failure (she has magical means of communicating the status of the line). 
* The party can try a medium Reason test to use the winch to pull the cable up. 
	* If Vinn is with them they can skip the test and she auto succeeds at it if prompted. 
	* If they succeed the noise is immense as it shakes off the remnants of the cars, but it does come up.
	* The end of the cable has half a runic circle around it, sliced clean through in a perfect line, much like the end of the cable itself. 
	* An appropriate lore check will reveal that the runes were configured to wait for the car to cross, then sever the line.

If Vinn is with the party, she’ll want to continue with them to Dimdale once she’s sent her status update. She can communicate the status of the line from here, but she’ll need to go get help to repair the line.

The remainder of the trip to Dimdale is uneventful.  The party can chat on the way if they want (can prompt with question below) or we can skip to their arrival.

* When the silence stretches too long, who breaks it first, and what do they say?
* After everything you’ve seen — the Fog, the demons, the crash — what part of it is still replaying in your head?
* You’ve survived what should’ve killed you. How do you feel about that — relieved, proud, or quietly unnerved?
* The air here feels different, lighter somehow. What do you notice for the first time now that you’re not running or climbing?
* The cairns that line the path are said to bring luck if you add a stone. Who does, and what silent thought accompanies the gesture?
* If Vinn is with the party, she keeps glancing back toward the line’s direction. What question do you ask her about her work or the mountain?
* Someone wonders aloud if the sabotage was meant for you — or if you were just in the wrong car. How does that land?
