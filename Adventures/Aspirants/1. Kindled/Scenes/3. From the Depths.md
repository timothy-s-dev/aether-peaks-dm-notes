The Roiling Fog. You can feel it already — a chill on your skin that’s more than cold air, a prickle that seems to crawl under your thoughts. 
>

# Tracking the Fog
Use a 4-segment clock to track how close the Fog gets to the PCs before they reach the ridge. It won't catch them here no matter what, but may start closer in the upcoming combat encounter, depending on how they do here.

## First Challenge: Finding a Path
Medium Intuition Test for one PC
* <=11 - As far as you can tell, there is no easy path, this is going to be a scramble no matter where you start. Tick the fog clock and take a bane on the next check.
* 12-16 - You find a path, but the start of it is a ways off, tick the fog clock.
* 17+ - You find a path, the start of which is a ways off, but you think you could jump and catch the ledge directly ahead to save time. Don’t tick the clock.

## Second Challenge: Cliffside Path
The mountain path has crumbled away here, leaving only a narrow ledge, slick with condensation.

Easy Agility Test for both PCs, they must pick who tries to go first
* <=11 - You make it across, but the path crumbles behind you - if anyone else needs to make it, they take a bane on their check.
* 12-16 - You make it across.
* 17+ - You make it across quickly - if both players get this result, or if one crits, don’t tick the fog clock. If neither player crits, and they don’t both get 17+, tick the fog clock.

## Third Challenge: Cresting the Ridge
The slope steepens sharply to a stone ridgeline above you. Wind howls across the face, scattering loose stones. The Fog is nearly upon you — only a final push remains. 

Medium Might Test for both PCs
* <=11 - You reach the ledge, but it crumbles under your fingers sending you falling back down. You’re uninjured, but have to spend precious time climbing again, tick the fog clock.
* 12-16 - You scale the ledge, but are pelted with falling pebbles as you climb, take 2 damage.
* 17+ - You scale the ledge safely.

# The Rest of the Party
When the players drag themselves over the ridge the ground levels out a bit.  Still a climb, but no longer a steep one, which is good, because it's kept their unconscious compatriots from rolling the rest of the way down the mountain.

Everyone seems to be alive, and can be helped up. Getting to this point alive grants the party one victory. Once everyone is being helped up, move to [[4. Firelight and Farewell]]
