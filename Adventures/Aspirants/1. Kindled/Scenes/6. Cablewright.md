As the party gets closer they can see a few things:
* The maintenance car is smaller than the one you were riding; built for a single passenger.
* From the edge of the path closest to the car you can just make out the symbol of the Cablewright's Guild stamped on one of the warped and rent panels.
* It's lodged in a crevice about fifty feet away across a steep rock face - there's no obvious path from here.
* The slope between is broken and treacherous, scatter with loose shale and patches of ice.

Crossing to the car is an easy Agility group check:

* <=11 - One of you loses your footing and slides several feet; whoever falls and whoever helps them are busy trying to get everyone back together while the others investigate the wreck.
* 12-16 - You make steady progress, but your arms are burning by the time you all reach the other side, and stones clatter below you. You’re beyond the reach of the demons for now, but who knows what else might have heard that…
* 17+ - The climb is treacherous, but you all make it without issue.

# The Cablewright's Car
* The car is hanging at an angle, one corner wedged into the rock, the other suspended in the open air by the remains of the groaning cable.
* The air hums faintly with residual magic, the smell of ozone sharp and metallic in your throat.
* Inside, shattered crystal lenses spark fitfully among the wreckage of tools.
* A figure lies slumped against the far wall - the cablewright, judging by her soot-streaked guild jacket and the shattered scrying lens still strapped to one arm.
* If they approach, her breathing is shallow and uneven, but detectable. 
* She stirs as they get close, and the car shudders - a deep metallic groan.  The cable won't hold long.

Any test the party wants to roll to extricate the engineer will successfully remove her from the car, but unless they do something clever (and probably roll well to boot) the car is going to fall shortly afterwards.

The Cablewright is a dwarven woman in engineer's work garb.  An easy Heal (Reason) check can help bring the engineer back to consciousness.  

## Vinn Berghish
* The Cablewright's name is Vinn Berghish
* She has been working the Dimdale-Stonehollow line for about 4 years, and been an engineer for most of her adult life.
* A routine scrying examination revealed something strange on the line, about 3/4 of the way to Stonehollow. 
* She took the car out to examine it up close, but the line broke before she got there. 
* As of the previous detailed inspection the line was in perfect condition - she’s proud of this, and offended greatly if it’s suggested the failure might be her fault in any way.

Assuming the party manages to avoid offending Vinn, any social tests that come up with her are made with a double boon after saving her life. If they openly suggest the failure might be her fault and don’t immediately recant they take a bane instead. 

If conscious, she will ask for help getting the rest of the way up the mountain. The remaining climb is a group Might check. If they crossed to the car they can use the cable to climb, giving them a boon. If they found Vinn and either she or they managed to get the climbing gear out of the pack they get another boon. 

<=11 - You make it up, but by the time you get there you’re ragged, your clothes are a mess, and you’ll be taking a bane on social checks until you can get cleaned up. (Not with Vinn) 
12-16 - You make it up to the top.
17+ - You make it up to the top, and find some of your lost gear along the way!
