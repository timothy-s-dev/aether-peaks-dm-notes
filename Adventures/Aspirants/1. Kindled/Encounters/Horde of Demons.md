For 3 PCs: 8 Pitlings, 12 Ensnarers, 1 Muceron
For 4 PCs: add 4 Pitlings, and 4 Ensnarers
For 5 PCs: Add 4 Pitlings and 1 Muceron

The battlemap either needs to be quite long, or else have obstacles I can move along it to simulate that length. The fight will be a running one, with the fog rising 4 squares towards them per round. It starts 8 squares away, -1 for each tick on the clock. The fog can be driven back with wind or fire abilities; either can clear it in a 2-3 square burst around the impact. If a hero is caught within the fog at the start of their turn they take 3 corruption damage. The fog will only advance for 4 rounds, after which it reaches “High Tide” and stops, though the demons make no such concession.

This is a medium encounter, worth 1 victory to the party
