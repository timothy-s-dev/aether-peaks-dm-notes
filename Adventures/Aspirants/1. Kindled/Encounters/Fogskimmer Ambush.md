For 3 PCs: 2x [[Fog Skimmers#^adult-tideskimmer-statblock|Adult Fog Skimmers]]
For 4 PCs: No change
For 5 PCs: +1 [[Fog Skimmers#^fledgling-fogskimmer-swarm-statblock|Fledgling Fog Skimmer Swarm]]

This battle takes place on a cliff side with multiple tiers. If the party failed the Agility check to approach the [[6. Cablewright|Cablewright's car]], one or two of them will start on a lower level.

Scaling up a level requires a Might or Agility power roll made as a maneuver:
* <11 - You fail to scale the wall, or scaling it ends your turn with you prone
* 12-16 - Scaling the wall ends your turn, or you are prone at the top of the wall
* 17+ - You reach the top just fine

PCs cannot be knocked all the way off the cliff - if they would be knocked off the lowest level they are instead left dangling, and must make the above scaling roll to get back up.
