When the party arrives in East Midport they find the cable-cars shut-down.  There have been signs of tampering, and after the failure of the Stonehollow-Dimdale line they aren't taking any risks.  If the party intends to complete their pilgrimage they'll have to find another way to cross.

This adventure should focus on the danger simple travel in this world entails, especially when the infrastructure build to mitigate it is not available to them.

I think the recommended route will involve climbing as high as possible on the Eastern peak, gliding across (and down) to the West side, then climbing a dangerous pass up from there.  

The terrain and wildlife are the primary adversaries here, but at some point they are also ambushed by Talon agents (maybe establish a "rival" party?).
