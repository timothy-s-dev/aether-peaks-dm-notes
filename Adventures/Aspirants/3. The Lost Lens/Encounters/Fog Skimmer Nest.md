Two potential encounters with the Fog Skimmers, depending on whether they're encountered in their nest (the harder encounter) or out and about in Ashenspire.

# Wandering Encounter
For 3 PCs: 2x [[Fog Skimmers#^adult-tideskimmer-statblock|Adult Fog Skimmers]]
For 4 PCs: No change
For 5 PCs: +1 [[Fog Skimmers#^fledgling-fogskimmer-swarm-statblock|Fledgling Fog Skimmer Swarm]]

The Fog Skimmers are wild animals, and aren't willing to die for their meal.  They will flee if low on HP.
# Nest Encounter
For 3-4 PCs: 1x Fog Skimmer Alpha
For 5 PCs: +1 Fledgling Fog Skimmer Swarm

This is the Fog Skimmer's nest, and the Alpha *will* die trying to protect it.  The swarm, if present, will still flee at low HP.
