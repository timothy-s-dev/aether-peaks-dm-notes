Two potential encounters with Rockworms.  For both encounters the rockworms are just defending territory - they won't give their lives for it.  If reduced to low HP they are likely to burrow down and flee.
# Wandering Group
For party of 3: 1x Rockworm, 8x Rockworm Larva
For party of 5: 2x Rockworm, 4x Rockworm Larva

# Boss Room
This encounter is worth two victories.
For party of 3: 1x Rockworm Alpha, 4x Rockworm Larva
For party of 4: 1x Rockworm Alpha, 8x Rockworm Larva
For party of 5: 1x Rockworm Alpha, 1x Rockworm, 4x Rockworm Larva
