When the party makes it through the gates into Stormcradle (and under the safety of the lightning towers) they are met by Caela - who is soaked.  She heard the aspirants from Stonehollow were expected to arrive soon, heard about the cable car line failing, and wants word of her friend Sir Alaric Vale.

She takes the party to her house, offers to put them up there in comfort, in exchange for news. On the way to her house, kept dry by some strange contraption she carries, she fills them in on recent happenings in Stormcradle - the aspirants from Ravenroost just left a few days ago, carrying a lens needed to repair a telescope in Starwatch Hold.  There are concerns that they may have run into trouble, as the most recent ravens from Starwatch Hold indicated they had yet to arrive, but should have by now. There were strange lights over Ashenspire a few days back, she's worried something happened to them there - no surprise, if they were brave or foolhardy enough to try and camp there. She's happy to explain what happened to Ashenpeak, and the rumors of ghosts and worse that waylay travelers there.

She grills them over dinner for news.  If they describe Sir Alaric Vale in detail, or describe him doing something wildly out of character, she may become confused and worried, and go fetch the painting from their room.  If not, dinner ends cordially and she has them shown to their rooms by one of her mechanical servants.

Once in the room they have a moment to get settled before one of them notices a painting on the wall - it's of their hostess and a heavy-set, red-headed man at some sort of trade show.  A plaque underneath the painting describes it as "Caela Orner (left) and Sir Alaric Vale (right) at the Unveiling of Coryur's Mechanical Servants"

The characters, if not the players, will immediately notice that's decidedly not the man who introduced himself as Sir Alaric Vale. If they bring this up to Caela she lands somewhere between terrified and pissed as all hell. She wants to leave immediately for Sir Normand Searle's house - he's the ranking Ember Guard member currently resident in the city.

If the party won't go with her she'll leave on her own.  (Though they may be able to talk her into waiting until morning if they have a good argument.)  If they don't mention it to her, but try to find an Ember Guard to report this to, they end up at Searle's house anyways.
