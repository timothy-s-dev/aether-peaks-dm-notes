#stub 

The Ravenroost Aspirants took shelter from a storm in Ashenspire a few days prior.  They had difficulty accessing the outbuilding and intact towers, and so took one of the ziplines from the broken Tower of Smoke to get into the second floor of the Tower of Cinders.

Once inside they were startled by the ghosts, and one of them dropped the lens through the opening into the bottom level, where it still lies, somehow undamaged.  They descended to retrieve it, but were beset by the ghosts and only Vigo Boffin managed to escape.

He is huddled in the house (which he broke into to avoid the towers), low on rations, and lower on sanity.  He's terrified of the party if they find him when they arrive, but can be calmed.  He'll explain what happened, and that he can't leave without the Lens, or all of this was for nothing.... He doesn't mention it unless they roll exceptionally well, but he also wants to retrieve an amulet worn by one of the other aspirants, his sister Esmee Boffin.  It's not magical, but was his mothers, and he doesn't feel he'll ever be able to return home without it, given he can't bring Esmee back....

There's a lot of survivor's guilt here - he didn't survive through bravery, or even cowardice, he just happened to be the one closest to an open window when the others fell.  He tried to stay, but couldn't.

The party can optionally try to talk him into just leaving it all, or offer to help retrieve it.
