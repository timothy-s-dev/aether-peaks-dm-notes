Sir Normand Searle is generally found at his home, as he both works and sleeps there, though he sometimes goes out for a drink at the Thin Hill Bar, meets fellow Ember Guard members at The Grove (a fancy restaurant), or walks the neighborhood when he needs to think.

He is happy to receive the Aspirants as a rule, though is less pleased if it means getting dragged out of bed in the middle of the night.  He is familiar with Caela Orner, they're both friends with Sir Alaric Vale, but isn't particularly close to her himself (despite living in Stormcradle he's skeptical of the lightning-powered tech).

He doesn't doubt the party's tale, once he's heard it, but suggests that it is an issue they should leave to the Ember Guard proper, and continue to focus on their pilgrimage. They have ways of finding their own; he'll make sure Sir Alaric Vale is found, and brought home safe if possible, and that if anyone has harmed him, that they are found too.

Caela doesn't love this, but also isn't an adventurer herself, and isn't inclined to go wandering over the mountains to look for him, and doesn't want to put the party in danger.
