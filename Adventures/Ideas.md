# Settlement Quests
## Highspire
* **Search for an Assassin** - A powerful figure has been assassinated in Highspire - find out who did it, and why. Then catch them.
## Mistport
* **Until Further Notice** - The Mistport cable cars are closed - can you find another way across?
## Splitstone
* **A Long Way Down** - A bridge in Splitstone failed, causing someone to fall to their death. Was it an accident, or murder? Or were demons responsible?
## Brightmirror
* **7 Years of Bad Luck** - Brightmirror needs a replacement mirror, but lacks the resources; they need assistance transporting the heavy materials from Skyforge in once piece.
## Skyforge
* **Retrieve the Cure** - The head artificer in Skyforge is dying - there are rumors that an alchemist in Fogreach could provide a cure, but the artificer is in no shape to travel. Bring back the cure, or the alchemist, and save the artificer.

## Greenloft
* **Pestilence** - Help investigate a bad crop in Greenloft that threatens the food supply of the entire range.

## Stormcradle
- **Lightning Thieves** – A rival settlement or rogue band is siphoning off storm energy from Stormcradle’s lightning towers. Discover who is responsible, and whether to stop them or broker a deal.
- **Crystal Gone Wild** – One of the crystal batteries storing lightning has begun to pulse uncontrollably. It threatens to shatter and release devastation — the PCs must either stabilize it, relocate it, or find a safe way to discharge it.

## Stonehollow
- **The Silent Song** – The great cavern where public assemblies are held has gone eerily silent. Acoustics are failing, voices don’t echo, and crops aren’t growing properly. Investigate what is corrupting the cave’s “resonance.”
- **Echoed Crimes** – Someone is using the cave’s echoes to impersonate townsfolk’s voices, sowing mistrust and false accusations. Find the trickster or source.

## Grainspire
- **Spoiled Harvest** – A fungal blight spreads through hydroponics; locals whisper it may be sabotage from rival farmers in Greenloft. Determine the true source before famine spreads.

## Mirewatch
- **Corpselight** – Corpses scavenged from the Fog return at night, glowing faintly and wandering the settlement. Are they possessed? Cursed? Or carrying something back from below?

## Dimdale
- **Cracks in the Shield** – A new fissure has opened in one of the ridges that keeps the Fog out. If not sealed or warded, the Fog will eventually flood the valley.

## Lanternreach
- **Lantern Festival** – Once a decade, a massive ceremonial lantern is meant to be lit and carried to Emberglass Peak. This time, the lantern has gone missing on the eve of the festival. Was it stolen, destroyed, or hidden?
- **Risky Research** - Lanternreach is experimenting with a new kind of magical lantern, but some are raising concerns that it may cause the same kind of disaster that befell Ashenspire - take one to a nearby peak and prove it's safe... or not

## Ravenroost
- **Messenger Lost** – A raven carrying a critical message never arrived. Track it down (was it killed by a predator? Captured? Or did the bird deliver the letter to the wrong person?).

# Ruin-Based Adventures
## Ashenspire
* **The Abandoned Artifact** - Something valuable was left behind in the haunted halls of Ashenspire, retrieve it.
- **The Last Beacon** – Rumors say one of the fire-beacons atop Ashenspire still sputters with eternal flame. Recover it for Lanternreach or the God Emperor’s temple.
- **Ghostly Bargain** – The spirits haunting Ashenspire whisper promises of secret safe routes through the Fog… but at what cost?

## Ironwell
- **Buried Alive** – Explorers digging in Ironwell discover sealed chambers containing survivors in stasis — or their twisted descendants. Should they be freed?
- **Iron Curse** – Black iron from Ironwell turns up in a market in Greenloft. Whoever is mining illegally is also spreading cursed metal.
- **Family Heirloom** - Someone left a valuable family heirloom in the hurried evacuation of Ironwell, and would like it returned.

# Travel / Inter-Settlement Hooks
- **Pilgrim Escort** – A group of pilgrims has gone missing between Thornhold and Emberglass Peak. The PCs must retrace their steps and uncover what happened.
- **Trade Dispute** – Skyforge accuses Iron Peaks of stealing ore shipments. Tensions could boil into violence unless proof is found.
- **Bridge of Glass** – An ancient span of crystal has been revealed at low Fog. Can it be crossed safely, or is it a demon trap?
- **Bad Information** - Several pilgrims have gone missing following maps and tide schedules provided by Starwatch Hold, who blames bad information from Fogwatch about the behavior of the fog. Who is at fault? What happened?

# Weird/Atmospheric Ones
- **Cloudrest Dreams** – People sleeping in Cloudrest claim to share the same dream, where they are flying over the Fog and seeing strange shapes below. Investigate whether the Fog itself is sending visions.
- **Goat’s Crown Horns** – A prize goat has grown horns of pure crystal. Scholars, priests, and thieves all want it for themselves. Protect it, sell it, or discover why it happened.
- **Fogreach Elixir** – A distilled mist turns out to be incredibly potent, granting visions or power — but also seems to attract demons. Decide whether to suppress or spread it.
