While the fog is dangerous to most creatures, some wildlife has adapted, if not to survive in the fog, then to take it's presence into account.

# Fog-Adapted Malice
**Cull the Weary** - 3 Malice
**Effect**: The creature's next attack against a Weakened, Bleeding, or Dazed creature gains an edge.

**Rising Tide** - 5 Malice
**Effect**: All fog on the map expands by 3 squares.

**Fog Breach** - 7 Malice
**Effect**: A new pool of fog appears (3x3 squares) centered on a target location within 8 squares of the current creature.
