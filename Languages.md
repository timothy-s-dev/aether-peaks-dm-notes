Most everyone on the Aether Peaks speaks Caelian, more often referred to as "Imperial" or "the Common Tongue".
# Regional Languages
Not super widely spoken, but still used for some formal meetings/documents, etc, largely a leftover from before the Fog, based on who settled there first. The western and central areas were mostly from the center of the original empire, and so don't generally have a second language.

- **Phaedran** (associated with the NW range, from Grainspire to Mirewatch)
- **Riojan** (associated with the northern range, from Lanternreach up to Skyforge and over to Fogreach)    
- **Vaniric** (associated with the eastern range, Dimdale over to Cloudrest and up to Goat's Crown)
- **Khemharic** (associated with the southern range, Iron Peaks and Fogwatch)
# Ancestral Languages
The world before the empire was less cosmopolitan, so most ancestries once had a language (or several) of their own. Most, however, have not seen use in a long time, and are nearly forgotten. These will appear later, in the "Dead Languages" section. The following still see some use:

- **Axiomatic** - language of the Memoneks, still used when they help with contracts, or between others of their own kind
- **Hyrallic** - language of the High Elves, used for formal documents and ceremonies associated with their old tie to the True Elves.
- **Kethaic** - language used by kobolds, bugbears, and goblins - rarely known by citizens of the Empire
- **Kuric** - language used by elementals - generally only known by elementalists or scholars
- **Tholl**/**Proto-Ctholl** - high/low variants of the same language, used by the various tiers of demons
- **Yllyric** - language of druids
- **Zaliac** - originally the language of the dwarves, now mostly found on schematics and similar documents related to mechanical things
# Dead Languages
These languages are no longer in regular use in the Empire, but since relics are still occasionally found or dug out of libraries containing text in them, scholars try to ensure they are not lost entirely.
- **The First Language** - the language of dragons, not that dragons are generally seen any more in the Empire
	- Truly ancient history, the roots of magic, ancient rituals, etc
- **Kalliak** - once the language of the orcs
	- The making of magical artifacts (that aren't weapons, or armor)
- **Khamish** - language of the beast-folk, generally lost to the fog
	- Nature magics, information about the wilds and the things that inhabit it
- **Khoursirian** - once a language of the polder, though often spoken by humans as well
	- Recipes, history, secrets
- **Variac** - language of the World Below, which has been generally cut off by the fog, though its possible tunnels in the mountains could lead that deep. 
	- Often dangerous to even read, detailing eldritch rituals
- **Rallarian** - language of a lost sub-species of dwarves, famous for creating and working legendary metals
	- Use for info about finding/working rare metals, and potentially some crafting of tools and weapons
